Moonlit Japan

Japan travel & culture, anime style

Moonlit Japan is an independent YouTube channel. Real photos of Japan turn into hand-painted anime scenes while Kaguya (a princess from the moon), Usagi (a cheeky moon rabbit) and Jiiya (a very strict butler) explore famous places, food, manners and travel tips.

Moonlit Japan Studio Tools

Moonlit Japan Studio Tools is a private, internal tool used only by the channel owner to manage the Moonlit Japan channel. It uses the YouTube API Services to upload and schedule the channel's own videos, to set their titles, descriptions, playlists, captions, thumbnails and AI-content disclosure, and to read the channel's own YouTube Analytics.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
